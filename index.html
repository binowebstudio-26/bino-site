Questions

Good to know.

How much does a website cost?

It depends on how many pages and features you need. After a short, free chat, I send you a clear written quote with no surprises. You only pay once you approve it.

How long does a website take?

Most sites are ready in 1 to 4 weeks. It depends on the size of the site and how quickly I get your photos and text.

What do I need to give you?

Your logo, photos, and the details of what you sell. If you don't have everything, that's okay. I'll send you a simple checklist and help with the rest.

Do I own my website?

Yes. Once the project is paid in full, the website and its content are yours, and your domain is always registered in your name.

Do I have to pay every month?

No. The care plan is optional. Without it, I set up your hosting in your name and hand everything over. You can always ask me for changes later.

Will my website show up on Google?

Every Standard and Custom site gets a basic Google setup: clear page titles, descriptions, and a Google Business Profile link. Nobody can promise the #1 spot, but you'll be set up the right way.

How does payment work?

Nothing is charged on this website. Choosing a plan just starts a free quote request. After a short chat, I send a written quote. When you're happy with it, you pay a 50% deposit through a secure online invoice (card or bank transfer), and the rest before your site goes live.

How does the care plan work, and how do I cancel?

The care plan is optional and only starts after your site launches. It's billed automatically once a month to the card you choose. You can cancel anytime by emailing hello@binostudio.com, or from the billing link in your receipt, and it stops at the end of that month.

Do you work with businesses outside Virginia?

Yes. Everything can be done over email, phone and video calls, so it doesn't matter where your business is.
