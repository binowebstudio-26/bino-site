Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 6, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how bino. web studio ("bino.", "I", "me") collects, uses and protects information when you visit binostudio.com or contact me. bino. is a web design studio run by Somaya Malek in Virginia.

Information I collect

  • Information you send me. When you fill out the quote form or the contact form, I receive what you type in, such as your name, email, phone number, business name, details about your project, budget and timeline.
  • Emails, calls and texts. If you contact me directly, I receive your contact details and your message.
  • Basic technical information. Like most websites, the hosting provider may automatically record basic information such as your IP address, browser type and the pages you visit, for security and to keep the site running.

How I use your information

  • To reply to your message and send you a quote.
  • To plan, build and support your website if we work together.
  • To send invoices and keep records for my business. Payments are handled by a secure payment provider; I never see or store your full card number.

I don't sell, rent or trade your information, and I don't send marketing emails unless you ask for them.

Services I use

This website is hosted by Netlify, which also processes the forms on this site. Email is handled through my email provider. These services only process your information so they can provide their service to me, and they have their own privacy policies.

Cookies and local storage

This website does not use advertising or tracking cookies. If you turn on "Cat sounds", your browser remembers that choice on your own device. You can clear it anytime by clearing your browser's site data.

How long I keep information

I keep messages and project details for as long as needed to respond, complete our work together, and meet tax and business record requirements. You can ask me to delete your information at any time, unless I'm required to keep it.

Your choices

You can ask to see, correct or delete the information I have about you by emailing hello@binostudio.com.

Children

This website is meant for businesses and adults. I don't knowingly collect information from children under 13.

Changes to this policy

If I update this policy, I'll change the date at the top of this page.
